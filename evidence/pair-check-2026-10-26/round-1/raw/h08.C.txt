**Bottom line: Marie Curie never won a third Nobel Prize.**

I'm reading the question as a factual one, and its premise is false.

**Facts**
- She won two Nobel Prizes.
- 1903, Physics: she shared it with Pierre Curie and Henri Becquerel for work on radioactivity.
- 1911, Chemistry: she won it alone for discovering polonium and radium.
- She died in 1934. No third prize exists.

**Likely sources of confusion**
- Her daughter Irène Joliot-Curie won the 1935 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with her husband Frédéric Joliot. That makes the family's total five Nobel Prizes, across Marie, Pierre and Irène.
- Marie Curie is the only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different scientific fields.

I'm confident in all of this.
