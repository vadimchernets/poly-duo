Bottom line: the fifth daughter is named Mary.

Reading chosen: the question is a riddle, and "Mary's father" means Mary is one of his five daughters.

Facts:
- The question lists four names: Nana, Nene, Nini, Nono.
- It says the father has five daughters.
- The father is described as Mary's father, so Mary is his daughter.

Conclusion: the four listed names plus Mary make five, so Mary is the fifth.

Not sure: the question doesn't say outright that Mary is his daughter. A different reading is possible, for example that Mary is the father's wife's friend, or that his fifth daughter is unnamed. I'm going with Mary because the riddle is built to point that way.
