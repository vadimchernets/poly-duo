**Final answer: 750 g of flour and 7.5 eggs. In practice, use 750 g of flour and 7–8 eggs.**

The scale factor is 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5.

- Flour: 300 g × 2.5 = 750 g
- Eggs: 3 × 2.5 = 7.5 eggs

Half an egg is awkward to measure. Beat one egg, then add about half of it (roughly 25 g). Or round to 8 medium eggs for a slightly richer result, or 7 for a slightly drier one. Rounding barely matters for most batters and doughs. For precision-sensitive bakes such as sponge cake, use the beaten-egg method.

Other ingredients (liquids, sugar, fat) scale by the same 2.5× factor. Leavening, spices and salt can be scaled slightly less. Baking time and pan size may also need adjusting.
