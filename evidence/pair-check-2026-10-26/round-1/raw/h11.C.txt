**1296 rectangles** (including 204 squares). Reading chosen: an 8×8 board of squares, so 9 horizontal and 9 vertical grid lines, counting every axis-aligned rectangle whose sides lie on those lines.

- Non-square rectangles: 1296 − 204 = 1092.

**Conclusion**
