**The average speed is 45 km/h.** The colleague's answer is correct.

Average speed is total distance divided by total time, not the mean of the two speeds.

- Time for the first leg: 60 / 30 = 2 h
- Time for the second leg: 60 / 90 = 2/3 h
- Total distance: 120 km
- Total time: 2 + 2/3 = 8/3 h
- Average speed: 120 / (8/3) = 45 km/h

The simple average of 30 and 90 would be 60 km/h. That is wrong here because the car spends more time at the slower speed. For equal distances, the harmonic mean applies: 2·30·90 / (30 + 90) = 45 km/h.
