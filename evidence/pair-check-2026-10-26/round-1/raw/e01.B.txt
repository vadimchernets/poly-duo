**The 2024 federal standard deduction for a single filer is $14,600.** The colleague's answer is correct.

- This is the amount for returns filed in 2025. It comes from Rev. Proc. 2023-34 and is $750 more than the 2023 figure of $13,850.
- A single filer who is 65 or older, or blind, adds $1,950 for each condition.
- For comparison, the 2024 amounts are $29,200 for married filing jointly and $21,900 for head of household. Married filing separately is $14,600.
- A dependent's deduction is the greater of $1,300 or earned income plus $450, capped at $14,600.
- The July 2025 law (the One Big Beautiful Bill Act) raised the standard deduction starting in tax year 2025. It did not change the 2024 figure.

Reading chosen: "single filer" means an unmarried taxpayer who is not a head of household, and the figure is the base amount before any age, blindness or dependent adjustments.
