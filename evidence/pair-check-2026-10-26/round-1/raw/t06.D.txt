Never: the Great Wall has never been visible from the Moon with the naked eye, so there is no such year. The claim is a myth.

- **Distance:** The Moon is about 384,000 km away. From there, the whole Earth looks like a small disk, and no individual human structure can be made out, the Wall included. It is long but only about 5–9 m wide.
- **Astronauts:** Apollo crews reported seeing no man-made structures from lunar distance.
- **Low Earth orbit:** Even from the ISS (about 400 km up), the Wall is very hard to pick out with the naked eye. It is similar in color to the terrain around it. Some astronauts say they have glimpsed it under ideal conditions, and others say they could not see it at all. Chinese astronaut Yang Liwei said he could not see it in 2003.
- **Origin of the myth:** The idea appeared in print before spaceflight. Ripley's "Believe It or Not!" (1932) and earlier writings claimed the Wall would be visible from the Moon or Mars.
