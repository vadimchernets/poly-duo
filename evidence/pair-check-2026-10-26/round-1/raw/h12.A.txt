**Bottom line: 26.4 hours.**

Assuming the pool starts empty and the hose flows continuously:

- Pool volume: \(12 \times 6 \times 1.5 = 108\ \text{m}^3\)
- \(108\ \text{m}^3 \approx 28{,}530\) US gallons
- Fill time: \(28{,}530 \div 18 \approx 1{,}585\) minutes
- \(1{,}585 \div 60 \approx 26.4\) hours
