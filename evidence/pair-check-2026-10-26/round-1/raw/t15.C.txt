**Bottom line: 15 different people.**

Reading chosen: "before Lincoln" means anyone who held the office before Lincoln's inauguration on March 4, 1861.

**Facts**
- Lincoln was the 16th presidency.
- Grover Cleveland's two non-consecutive terms are only a factor after Lincoln, so they don't change this count.
- Lincoln's 15 predecessors, in order: Washington, John Adams, Jefferson, Madison, Monroe, John Quincy Adams, Jackson, Van Buren, William Henry Harrison, Tyler, Polk, Taylor, Fillmore, Pierce, Buchanan.

**Conclusion**
- Each of these 15 held the office once and was a distinct person, so the count is 15.

**Caveat (not about the count)**
- Some count John Hanson and other presidents of the Congress under the Articles of Confederation. That reading is non-standard, since they weren't U.S. Presidents under the Constitution. If you included them, the number would be larger. I haven't verified how many that would add.
