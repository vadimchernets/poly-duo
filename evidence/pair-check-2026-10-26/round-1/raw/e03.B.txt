**Details**
- The limit was $7,000 for people under 50. It was $6,500 in 2023.
- People aged 50 or older could add a $1,000 catch-up, for $8,000 in total.
- The contribution cannot exceed taxable compensation for the year. A person who earned less than $7,000 is limited to what they earned.
- Roth IRA contributions phase out at higher incomes. For 2024 the range was $146,000–$161,000 for single filers and $230,000–$240,000 for married couples filing jointly.
- Traditional IRA deductibility can be reduced if you or your spouse is covered by a workplace plan.
- The deadline for 2024 contributions was April 15, 2025.
