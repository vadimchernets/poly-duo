Exceptions:
- **Leap second:** a UTC day with one has 86,401 seconds. None have been added since 2016.
- **Daylight-saving change:** a local day can have 82,800 or 90,000 seconds.
- **Sidereal day:** about 86,164 seconds.

Use 86,400 unless you need one of those cases.
