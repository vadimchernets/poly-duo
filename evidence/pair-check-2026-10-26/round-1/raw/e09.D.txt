**80 mg of alcohol per 100 ml of blood** is the legal driving limit in England. The same limit applies in Wales and Northern Ireland. Scotland has been lower since December 2014, at 50 mg per 100 ml.

The colleague's figure is correct. The limit is set in the Road Traffic Act 1988 and is a prosecution threshold for driving or being in charge of a vehicle. Equivalent limits are 35 µg per 100 ml of breath and 107 mg per 100 ml of urine. Because impairment starts at lower levels, the safest approach is not to drink and drive. Some drivers, such as those with a drink-driving conviction or in specific commercial roles, may face stricter rules or penalties.
