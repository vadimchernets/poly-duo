**Marie Curie did not win a third Nobel Prize. She won two, and the colleague's answer is correct.**

- **1903, Physics:** shared with Pierre Curie and Henri Becquerel, for work on radioactivity.
- **1911, Chemistry:** won alone, for discovering polonium and radium.

She was the first person to win Nobel Prizes in two different sciences, and the first woman to win a Nobel Prize. A third prize is a common mix-up. Her daughter Irène Joliot-Curie won the 1935 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with her husband Frédéric. That gives the Curie family five Nobel Prizes in all, counting Pierre's share of the 1903 prize.
