**Jane Austen wrote *Pride and Prejudice*.**

- It was first published anonymously on 28 January 1813 in London by Thomas Egerton. The title page said only "By the Author of *Sense and Sensibility*."
- Austen was named as the author after her death in 1817. Her brother Henry Austen identified her in his 1818 biographical notice.
- An earlier version, titled *First Impressions*, was drafted in 1796–1797. She revised it heavily before publication.

The attribution is not disputed.
