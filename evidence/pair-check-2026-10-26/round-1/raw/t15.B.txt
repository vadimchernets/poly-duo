Reading chosen: "before Lincoln" means anyone who held the office before his inauguration on March 4, 1861.

Lincoln was the 16th presidency, so he had 15 predecessors: Washington, John Adams, Jefferson, Madison, Monroe, John Quincy Adams, Jackson, Van Buren, William Henry Harrison, Tyler, Polk, Taylor, Fillmore, Pierce and Buchanan.

Grover Cleveland's two non-consecutive terms are the reason later presidency numbers exceed the number of people. They come after Lincoln, so they don't affect this count. Before Lincoln, presidencies and people match one-to-one.

Some people also count the presidents of the Continental Congress or the Articles of Confederation, such as John Hanson. They weren't presidents under the Constitution, so the standard answer excludes them.
