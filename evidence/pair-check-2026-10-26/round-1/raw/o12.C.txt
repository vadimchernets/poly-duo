**1989.**

Reading chosen: "fall" means the opening of the border crossings on the night of 9 November 1989.

**Facts**
- On 9 November 1989, East Germany announced relaxed travel rules. Crowds went to the crossings, and the guards opened them that night.
- Much of the wall was torn down over the following months. Germany formally reunified on 3 October 1990.

**Conclusion**
- If you mean the day the wall stopped working as a barrier, the answer is 1989.
- If you mean when it was fully dismantled, that was later. Official demolition began in June 1990 and went on into 1991, with some sections kept as memorials.
