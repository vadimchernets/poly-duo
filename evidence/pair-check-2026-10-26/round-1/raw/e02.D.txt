**The 2024 US federal annual gift tax exclusion is $18,000 per recipient.** The colleague's answer is correct.

- Each donor can give up to $18,000 to any number of recipients in 2024 without filing a gift tax return (Form 709) or using any of their lifetime exemption.
- A married couple can "split" gifts, which allows $36,000 per recipient. Gift-splitting usually requires filing Form 709, even if no tax is due.
- Gifts above $18,000 to one person aren't taxed right away. They must be reported and count against the lifetime exemption, which was $13.61 million per person for 2024.
- Some payments aren't gifts for this purpose, and don't count toward the $18,000. These include tuition or medical expenses paid directly to the institution or provider, and gifts to a spouse who is a US citizen (unlimited marital deduction).
- The exclusion rose to $19,000 for 2025.
