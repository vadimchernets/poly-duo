Jane Austen wrote "Pride and Prejudice".

**Facts**
- It was first published anonymously on 28 January 1813 in London by Thomas Egerton. The title page said only "By the Author of *Sense and Sensibility*."
- Austen's authorship became publicly known later, after her death in 1817. Her brother Henry Austen named her in his biographical notice, published in 1818.
- She drafted an earlier version, titled "First Impressions", in 1796–1797. She revised it heavily before publication.

**Conclusion**
There is no real dispute over the attribution. Jane Austen is the author.
