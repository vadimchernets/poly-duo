**Bottom line: 1/8** (27/216).

**Facts**
- Total outcomes: 6³ = 216, all equally likely.
- Ordered triples (a,b,c) with a+b+c = 10 and each die from 1 to 6, grouped by multiset:
  - {1,3,6}: 6 orderings
  - {1,4,5}: 6
  - {2,2,6}: 3
  - {2,3,5}: 6
  - {2,4,4}: 3
  - {3,3,4}: 3
  - Total: 6 + 6 + 3 + 6 + 3 + 3 = 27
- Cross-check by symmetry: sum 11 also has 27 outcomes, and 10 and 11 are the two most likely sums after 10.5. (The known counts for sums 3 to 10 are 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 25, 27.) These add up consistently to 108 for sums 3 to 10, which is half of 216.

**Conclusion**
P = 27/216 = 1/8 = 12.5%.
