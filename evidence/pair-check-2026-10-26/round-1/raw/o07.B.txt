**212 °F** (100 °C) at sea level, assuming standard atmospheric pressure (1 atm = 101.325 kPa) and pure water.

- Real sea-level pressure changes with the weather, so the actual boiling point shifts by a fraction of a degree.
- The boiling point falls roughly 1 °F for every ~500 m of altitude.
- Dissolved solutes such as salt raise it slightly.
