**Sally has 1 sister.**

**Reading chosen:** all the brothers share the same two parents (full siblings), and Sally is a girl.

**Facts:**
- Each brother has 2 sisters. Sally is one of them.
- The other sister is a girl besides Sally.
- Sally cannot count herself as her own sister, so her sisters are the girls in the family other than her. The family has 2 girls in total.

**Conclusion:** Sally has 1 sister. A common wrong answer is 2, which comes from forgetting that Sally is one of the brothers' 2 sisters.

**Not sure about:** half-siblings. If some brothers had different parents, the count could differ. The question doesn't say, so I assumed they are full siblings.
