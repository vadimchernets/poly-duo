31.

Trailing zeros come from factors of 10, each requiring a pair \(2 \times 5\). There are more factors of 2 than 5, so count factors of 5:

\[
\left\lfloor \frac{125}{5} \right\rfloor+
\left\lfloor \frac{125}{25} \right\rfloor+
\left\lfloor \frac{125}{125} \right\rfloor
=25+5+1=31.
\]

Therefore, \(125!\) has **31 trailing zeros**.
