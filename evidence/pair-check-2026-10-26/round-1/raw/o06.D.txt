Two caveats:
- Leap seconds: a day with a leap second inserted in UTC has 86,401 seconds. The last one was in 2016, and the practice is due to be discontinued by 2035.
- Daylight saving: in local time, a day can have 82,800 or 90,000 seconds on the days clocks change.
