Since \(14^3 = 2744 \equiv 744 \pmod{1000}\), and for every \(n \ge 3\),

\[
14^{n+100} \equiv 14^n \pmod{1000}.
\]

Thus,

\[
14^{884} \equiv 14^{84} \pmod{1000}.
\]

Repeated squaring:

\[
14^4 \equiv 416,\quad
14^8 \equiv 56,\quad
14^{16} \equiv 136,\quad
14^{64} \equiv 296 \pmod{1000}.
\]

Therefore,

\[
14^{84}=14^{64}14^{16}14^4
\equiv 296\cdot136\cdot416
\equiv \boxed{376}\pmod{1000}.
\]
