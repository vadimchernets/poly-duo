**13 August 2169 is a Sunday.**

Reading chosen: the proleptic Gregorian calendar, with the date as day 13, month August, year 2169.

**Facts (checked two independent ways):**
- Doomsday method: the anchor day for 2100–2199 is Sunday. Year 69 gives 5 + 9 + 2 = 16, and 16 mod 7 = 2, so the 2169 doomsday is Tuesday. 8 August is a doomsday, so 8 August is a Tuesday and 13 August is five days later, a Sunday.
- Day count: 1 January 2000 was a Saturday. From it to 1 January 2169 is 169×365 + 42 leap days (43 leap years from 2000 to 2168, minus 2100) = 61,727 days. 61,727 mod 7 = 1, so 1 January 2169 is a Sunday. 13 August is day 225 of the year, which is 224 days later, exactly 32 weeks, so it is again a Sunday.

**Conclusion:** Sunday. The two methods agree, and I have no doubt about the result.
