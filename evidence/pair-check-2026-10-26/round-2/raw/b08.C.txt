**14077 days**

Reading chosen: the end date counts and the start date does not, so the answer is the plain difference between the two dates.

Facts:
- From 15 June 2010 to 15 June 2048 is 38 years, which is 38 × 365 = 13870 days.
- The leap days (29 Feb) in that span fall in 2012, 2016, 2020, 2024, 2028, 2032, 2036, 2040, 2044 and 2048. That is 10 extra days, giving 13880.
- From 15 June to 29 December 2048 (a leap year) is 197 days. Cross-check: 29 Dec is day 364 of 2048 and 15 June is day 167, and 364 − 167 = 197.

Conclusion: 13880 + 197 = 14077.
