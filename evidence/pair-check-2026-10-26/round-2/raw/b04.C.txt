**17152 days.**

Reading chosen: the plain difference between the two dates (the end date is included, the start date is not).

**Facts**
- From 13 Sep 1963 to 13 Sep 2010 is 47 years, which is 47 × 365 = 17155 days plus the leap days.
- The leap days (29 Feb) in that span fall in 1964, 1968, …, 2008. That is 12 leap years, and 2000 is among them because it is divisible by 400.
- So 13 Sep 1963 to 13 Sep 2010 is 17155 + 12 = 17167 days.
- 29 Aug 2010 is 15 days before 13 Sep 2010 (2 days to 31 Aug, then 13 more).

**Conclusion**
17167 − 15 = **17152 days**.
