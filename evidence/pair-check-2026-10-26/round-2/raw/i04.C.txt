**Answer: 204**

Reading chosen: "divisible by 4 or by 6" is inclusive or, and "not by 9" applies to the whole union.

**Facts (inclusion–exclusion on 1..733):**
- Multiples of 4: ⌊733/4⌋ = 183
- Multiples of 6: ⌊733/6⌋ = 122
- Multiples of both, i.e. of lcm(4,6) = 12: ⌊733/12⌋ = 61
- Divisible by 4 or 6: 183 + 122 − 61 = 244

**Remove those also divisible by 9:**
- Divisible by 4 and 9 means a multiple of 36, and by 6 and 9 means a multiple of 18.
- Every multiple of 36 is also a multiple of 18, so the union is just the multiples of 18: ⌊733/18⌋ = 40.

**Conclusion:** 244 − 40 = **204**
