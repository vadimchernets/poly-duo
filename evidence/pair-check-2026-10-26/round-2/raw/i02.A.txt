**1134**

Divisible by 4 or 6:

\[
\left\lfloor\frac{4080}{4}\right\rfloor+
\left\lfloor\frac{4080}{6}\right\rfloor-
\left\lfloor\frac{4080}{12}\right\rfloor
=1020+680-340=1360.
\]

Among these, numbers divisible by 9 must be divisible by \(\operatorname{lcm}(6,9)=18\), giving

\[
\left\lfloor\frac{4080}{18}\right\rfloor=226.
\]

Therefore:

\[
1360-226=\boxed{1134}.
\]
