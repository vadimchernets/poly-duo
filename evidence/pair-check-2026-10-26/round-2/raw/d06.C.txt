Reading chosen: the proleptic Gregorian calendar, with no reforms assumed beyond 2199.

**Facts**
- 20 November 2000 was a Monday (known anchor).
- From 20 Nov 2000 to 20 Nov 2199 is 199 years.
- Leap days in that span: 49 leap years from 2004 to 2196, minus 2100 (not a leap year in the Gregorian calendar), gives 48.
- Total days: 199 × 365 + 48 = 72,683. Since 72,683 mod 7 = 2, the date falls 2 weekdays after Monday.

**Cross-check (Doomsday rule)**
- The century anchor for the 2100s is Sunday.
- For year 99: ⌊99/12⌋ = 8, remainder 3, ⌊3/4⌋ = 0, so the shift is 8 + 3 + 0 = 11, which is 4 mod 7. Sunday + 4 = Thursday, so 2199's doomsday is Thursday.
- 7 November is a doomsday date, so it is a Thursday. 14 and 21 November are also Thursdays, which makes 20 November a Wednesday.

Both methods agree. I have no uncertainty about this result.
