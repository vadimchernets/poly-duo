**The ball costs 1/30 of a dollar, about 3.33 cents ($0.0333…).**

Reading chosen: the bat costs $1.00 more than *twice* the ball's price.

Let the ball cost x. Then the bat costs 2x + 1.00.

x + (2x + 1.00) = 1.10, so 3x = 0.10, so x = 0.10/3 ≈ $0.0333.

Check: the bat costs 2(0.0333…) + 1.00 = $1.0666…, and 0.0333… + 1.0666… = $1.10. ✓

In practice, prices are rounded to whole cents. No whole-cent price satisfies the conditions exactly. The exact answer is 3⅓ cents.
