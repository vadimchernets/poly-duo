**253 people.**

Interpretation: “people” means other people, excluding you.

For \(n\) people, the probability that nobody shares your fixed birthday is:

\[
\left(\frac{364}{365}\right)^n
\]

Thus:

\[
1-\left(\frac{364}{365}\right)^n \ge 0.5
\]

The smallest integer satisfying this is \(n=253\). This differs from the classic birthday problem (23 people), which asks whether *any pair* shares a birthday.
