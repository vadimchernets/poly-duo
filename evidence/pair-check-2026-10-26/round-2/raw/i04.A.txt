**204 integers.**

Divisible by 4 or 6:

\[
\left\lfloor\frac{733}{4}\right\rfloor+
\left\lfloor\frac{733}{6}\right\rfloor-
\left\lfloor\frac{733}{12}\right\rfloor
=183+122-61=244.
\]

Among these, those divisible by 9 must be divisible by \(\operatorname{lcm}(6,9)=18\) or \(\operatorname{lcm}(4,9)=36\). Since every multiple of 36 is already a multiple of 18, their number is

\[
\left\lfloor\frac{733}{18}\right\rfloor=40.
\]

Therefore:

\[
244-40=\boxed{204}.
\]
