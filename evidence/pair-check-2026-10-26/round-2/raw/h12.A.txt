No, it does not follow.

Facts:

- No cats are dogs.
- Some dogs are not pets.

These premises establish the existence of at least one non-pet dog, but they do not establish that any cats exist. Even if cats do exist, nothing states whether they are pets.

Counterexample: there are no cats, and one dog that is not a pet. Both premises are true, while “some cats are not pets” is false. Therefore, the inference is invalid.
